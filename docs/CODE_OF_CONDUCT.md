# Code of Conduct

This is an adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which is licensed CC BY 4.0.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behavior that helps: being kind and patient, respecting other views, giving and accepting
feedback gracefully, owning mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks
- harassment in public or private
- publishing someone's private information without permission
- asking for or sharing pirated game files, built files, or ways to get around a license
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions, and will explain why when appropriate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@SSMG64](https://github.com/SSMG64), through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/SSMG64/MCJavaPSV/security/advisories/new). All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** A private note and a request to change, for unprofessional behavior.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning for a single serious incident or repeated behavior, with a
   period of no interaction.
3. **Temporary ban.** For serious violations or sustained inappropriate behavior.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward people.
